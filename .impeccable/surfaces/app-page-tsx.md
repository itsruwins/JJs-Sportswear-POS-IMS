# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Customer-first; the staff/owner side has its own section lower on the page. Primary action: create an account (username-based). Secondary: browse the shop. Key line: "Design it. Track it. Wear it." Light theme with dark mode from the system setting. Code-led build, no comps.

Visitors: team captains, schools, and organizations on phones in Legazpi, plus walk-in apparel buyers. Proof on hand: none; every order, product, and stock figure on the page is sample data labeled as such. No prices, counts, ratings, or testimonials.

Sections: hero scoreboard; how custom orders work (the real flow: design or AI draft, staff acceptance, at least 50% down payment via GCash or cash, print, cut, sew, inspect, ready, balance, pickup); AI design studio teaser; live catalog preview with size and stock; for the team (POS, inventory, low-stock alerts, payment verification, reports); closing CTA; footer with marked placeholders for shop details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every custom order is a game in progress, kept on the hand-flipped scoreboard of a barangay covered court and rebuilt with Apple precision. Refuses the category default of a centered headline over a device mockup and a bento grid.

OWN-WORLD: Court-green painted board panels (one saturated green owns the instrument surfaces), true off-white flip cards with near-black condensed numerals, shot-clock amber reserved for live and low states, neutral ground tinted toward green. Archivo across widths: extra-condensed black for board numerals and display, normal width for reading. Strict cell module, hinged flip cards, lamp dots, ruled bands, translucent nav. Stock state reads as card state: lit, amber for low, blank for sold out.

STORY: Visitors see a sample order flip from pending to completed and understand they can follow their own kit the same way. They see the steps, the AI studio, and live sizes, believe the shop runs on one honest system, and claim a username.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Translucent nav with the J&J wordmark and a Create account button. The headline "Design it. Track it. Wear it." is set large and left-aligned above a full-width court-green scoreboard: HOME team name and piece count on the left, a split-flap status word in the center, the order number and ready date on the right, four period lamps (Design, Down payment, Production, Pickup), and a size-run row. The board's bottom control strip holds the working username field and Create account, with Browse the shop as the secondary action.

FORM: Liga Scoreboard, position 7 of 7 on the grounded list (roster sheet, jersey numerals, job-order stub, sublimation sheet, liga tarpaulin, court lines, scoreboard). Seed key 35e186aa. Raises: continuous timestamped ruled band (Daylight Section); one instrument per viewport (Ikebana Ma); press travel on every control (CD-ROM Console); stock as card illumination (Signboard Canyon); size run as identical damped cells (VU Meter Bridge); strict cell module (ASCII). Signature interaction: tapping a period lamp flips the board to that stage, interruptible mid-flip; it auto-advances only while visible and never under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
